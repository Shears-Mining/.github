# Shears Mining – keeping small SHA-256 coins alive

We run a solo and merge-mining pool for Bitaxe and home miners, and we look after the **open-source code of the small SHA-256 coins** we mine.

**What you'll find here**

- 🗄️ **Preserved coins** – every coin we mine is forked here first, so its source, history and releases can't disappear when its developers move on.
- 🛡️ **[Shears StallGuard](https://github.com/Shears-Mining/stallguard)** – ASERT difficulty with a stall rescue and a floor, so a big miner leaving can't freeze a small chain. Free to use (MIT).
- ⛓️ **Merge-mining (AuxPoW) upgrades** – letting Bitcoin miners secure small coins at no extra cost.
- 🔧 **Community releases** for abandoned coins – tested on private test chains, with Windows, Linux and Raspberry Pi wallets, full source, checksums, and credit to the original developers.
- 🔍 **Infrastructure** – public nodes and [block explorers](https://sha256-mining.shears.co.uk/explorers.php) for coins that have lost theirs.

How Shears Mining works

Shears Mining is a small, independent SHA-256 pool run from rural (In the middle of nowhere) England, on off-grid solar power. Our hashrate follows the weather: more on sunny days, less in winter and fog.

At the time of writing we have about x50 Bitaxe Gamma 601, and a couple of nerd qaxe (4 times the hash of a bitaxe). We are currently involved in expanding our solar array, to add another 18x 300w panels, to give us more power in winter, those dull days, but i'm not sure that will be enough, but lets see !

Open by default. Every change we make to a coin is published on our GitHub with full source code, release notes and test results. If you want to know what we’re doing with a coin, that’s where to look. There’s no private or “insider” information.

Everyone hears at once. New releases, fork heights and fixes are announced on GitHub, Bitcointalk and our Discord at the same time. Nobody gets advance notice.

Non-custodial. Pool rewards go straight to each miner’s own wallet in the block itself. We never hold your coins.

We mine what we support. We mine the coins we look after, so we hold some of them. We don’t give price predictions or trading advice, and we don’t do private deals.

Handing back. Where we’ve taken over a coin whose team has gone quiet, we’re happy to hand everything back if the original developers return.

Questions? Ask in our Discord.

*A coin of yours stuck, or its developer gone quiet? Open an issue on the coin's repository here, or ask on Discord.*
